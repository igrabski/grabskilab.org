Isabella N. Grabski, PhD

Principal Investigator

Isabella Grabski is an incoming Assistant Professor at the Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia, in the Center for Computational and Genomic Medicine, and the University of Pennsylvania, in the department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine. She received her PhD in Biostatistics from Harvard University, where she was co-advised by Dr. Rafael Irizarry and Dr. Giovanni Parmigiani, and completed postdoctoral training at the New York Genome Center under the supervision of Dr. Rahul Satija and Dr. David Knowles. Previously, she received a B.S.E in Chemical and Biological Engineering from Princeton University.
