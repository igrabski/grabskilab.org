Isabella N. Grabski, PhD

Principal Investigator

Isabella Grabski is an incoming Assistant Professor at Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia and the University of Pennsylvania. She develops statistical and machine learning methods for genomics, with a focus on extracting biological insight from large-scale and underused data resources. She received her PhD in Biostatistics from Harvard University and completed postdoctoral training at the New York Genome Center.
