The Grabski Lab

The Grabski Lab develops statistical and machine learning methods to uncover biological mechanisms from large-scale genomic and imaging data. We leverage advances in single-cell genomics, high-throughput perturbation screens, and imaging technologies to understand how molecular processes shape cellular states and complex biological phenotypes in health and disease.
